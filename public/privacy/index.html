Last updated October 13, 2026 - Privacy policy

This policy covers Gilad Barnea Automation, the app that connects my WhatsApp Business number to Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. Gilad Barnea runs it, as a sole proprietor in Israel. Contact: gilad@gilad-barnea.com

What data the app receives

When you write to my WhatsApp Business number, Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform sends the app your messages, the media you send, your phone number and WhatsApp profile name, message times, and delivery statuses. It also receives the messages that I send you from the WhatsApp Business app, and, when I connect the number, up to six months of our past one-to-one chats. It receives no group chats.

Why

To keep a record of my conversations with clients, and to let my own software read them, so that I can answer you on time and remember what we agreed. The app does not send you advertising, and it does not use Meta's AI.

Where it is stored, and who sees it

The data is stored only on my own computer, in Israel. Software that I run reads it to help me work, and it may send text to the AI model providers that this software uses, only to process it for me. I do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone else.

How long

I keep the data until I decide to delete it, or until you ask me to delete it.

Your rights

You may ask to see the data I hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Write to gilad@gilad-barnea.com, and I will answer within 30 days.

Data deletion instructions

To delete your data, send an email to gilad@gilad-barnea.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the phone number you write to me from. I will delete your messages and media from the app's storage within 30 days, and I will confirm by email. Messages that remain in your own WhatsApp and in my WhatsApp Business app are not affected. These instructions are also on their own page.
