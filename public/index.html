I build software that removes the slow, repetitive, manual work small businesses put up with every day, so the hours it eats go back to the work that actually moves the business.

Every business runs on a handful of processes nobody chose. They accumulated.

Somebody re-types the same numbers into a second system. Somebody chases the same approval by phone every week. Somebody keeps the real state of things in a spreadsheet that only they understand.
‍
These processes are rarely a technology problem. They are a fit problem. Off-the-shelf tools are built for a generic business, and every real business is specific.

So I do not start with software. I start by sitting with you and mapping how the work actually moves: who touches what, where it stalls, and which steps exist only because they always have.

Then I build the smallest thing that removes the most pain, put it in front of you, and we adjust it together until it fits.

The result is usually unglamorous and quietly valuable: a process that used to take a morning now takes a minute, and nobody has to remember to do it.
‍
I work solo and directly with you, from the first conversation through to the last handover. No account managers, no discovery deck, and no passing you along to a team you have never met. You always know who built each piece, and why it works the way it does.
